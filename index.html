sandwichestime

A modern take on Hayhoe's sandwich task. You'll make a peanut butter and jelly sandwich in a virtual kitchen while your hands, gaze and actions are logged.

Controls
Mouse movemove the active hand
Clickgrasp / release
Tabswitch active hand (left / right)
Space (hold)press down: spread, scoop, cut, wipe
Wheeltwist a lid you hold / raise or lower a held object
F, Q / Eflip held object, rotate held object
A / D, ← / →step along the counter
Right-draglook around
Bellring it (grasp it) when the sandwich is done

Webcam. Hold your hands up in front of the camera, palms toward it. Each virtual hand goes to wherever your real hand points on the screen: up is farther away on the counter (or up onto the shelves).

Pinch thumb + indexgrasp; open the fingers to let go
Push toward the screenlower what you hold (press, spread, dip); pull back to lift
Rotate your handturn what you hold; unscrew a lid (counter-clockwise, about two strokes)
Turn your hand overflip a slice
Hand at the screen edgestep sideways (or A / D)
C / Spacehide the camera preview / press down without pushing

Pinch the open end of the bread bag to take a slice; pinch the closed end to move the bag. The first slice out is the heel.

Headset. Pinch or squeeze; everything is 1:1 scale.
