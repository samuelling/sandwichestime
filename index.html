sandwichestime

A modern take on Hayhoe's sandwich task. You'll make a peanut butter and jelly sandwich in a virtual kitchen while your hands, gaze and actions are logged.

Controls
Mouse movemove the active hand
Clickgrasp / release
Tabswitch active hand (left / right)
Space (hold)press down: spread, scoop, cut, wipe
Wheeltwist a lid you hold / raise or lower a held object
F, Q / Eflip held object, rotate held object
A / D, ← / →step along the counter
Right-draglook around
Bellring it (grasp it) when the sandwich is done

Webcam: pinch thumb and index to grasp, roll your wrist to twist or flip. Headset: pinch or squeeze; everything is 1:1 scale.

Assets: CC0 photoscans from Poly Haven. Data stays in your browser unless you set an upload endpoint.
